## Status And Evidence Rules

- Item IDs are stable. Do not renumber an accepted item; add a successor item when scope changes materially.
- `Accepted` means the stated acceptance condition has evidence. `Partial` means a real path exists but the complete condition does not. `Missing` means the user workflow is absent. `Deferred` is allowed only with an explicit product decision.
- Repository evidence and live/environment evidence are separate. One does not imply the other.
- `Not required` in the environment column means the behavior is deterministic and fully exercisable in the repository. `Pending` means an environment-dependent claim must not be described as shipped or externally validated.
- Roll-up views may summarize the highest completed evidence stage as `Not started`, `Scaffolded`, `Behavior implemented`, `Repository tested`, or `Native/live verified`. This ladder does not replace item status or the evidence columns in this matrix.
- `Native/live verified` requires the complete environment-dependent acceptance for that item; a partial smoke does not advance the whole item to that stage. An environment-independent item can be `Accepted` at `Repository tested` when its environment evidence is `Not required`.
- Progress is reported by accepted item IDs, never by a completion percentage.
- From R3 onward each row includes `Depends on`. R0–R2 keep their historical columns. An item has one owner phase or the unnumbered Deferred queue.
- Semantic settings are accepted on their behavior-owner rows. `PREF-01` supplies the durable preference mechanism and categorized Preferences surface only.
- `APP-01` semantic work depends on `SGF-07` and must pass its shared open/replace semantic gate in R5. `REL-04` is only the additional R11 final-acceptance gate for platform association and installed-live evidence; the item stays unaccepted until that gate. `APP-02` depends on `SGF-07` and the passed R5 `APP-01` semantic gate, never final `APP-01` Accepted.
- R10 and R11 numbers express delivery priority, not a hard dependency; either may proceed after its real prerequisites.
- Deferred items never become numbered-phase exit criteria until they are explicitly started.
- Release rows further separate repository release evidence, release-environment evidence, and installed-live evidence. Platform admission is independent. Canonical Artifacts are Windows NSIS plus portable, macOS DMG, and Linux AppImage.
- Ticket 09 routed `SET-NEXT-MOVE`, `SET-WINRATE-GRAPH`, `SET-SUBBOARD`, and `SET-MAIN-PANEL` extras to owner domains. Tickets 10–14 assigned them no independent stable Parity ID. Ticket 16 must close that disposition-to-item gap. Those routes are not Accepted, Deferred, or absorbed into `ANA-04`, `UI-01`, or `PREF-01`.
- `GUIDE-01` keeps its stable ID and Missing status. Ticket 09 named the domain-04 auto-analyze education path as its producer; Ticket 11 Abandoned `CAP-04-ANA-08` automatic current-game analysis. That abandoned path cannot satisfy `GUIDE-01`. Ticket 16 must close the producer replacement or disposition. No substitute tip is assigned here.
